Ghost bars: a 1D trendline on TSLA hourly candles

Capital.com TSLA candles, last 10 daily bars, with simulated holes (switchable). The line runs through the Nov 2 and Nov 3 highs and is touched again by Nov 10's high, which is simulated. The top panel is the chart as it is today. The bottom one pads every day to the same number of slots with empty ghost bars, so the daily line is straight. Hover any panel to read the line at that moment in all three.

What to look for. In the ghost panel the line is one straight stroke and every day opens at the daily value. In the top panel, Smooth and Steps hit the same daily values at each day's open; Straight through anchors drifts, because the anchor candles sit at different hours of their days.
Where the ghosts are. A slot is one chart period from the daily open (24 a day on 1H, 6 on 4H). A slot with no candle is a ghost: early closes, late opens, gaps. Weekends are not ghosts, since no daily bar covers them. "Simulated" removes candles to fake an early close, a late open, a halt and a short day; "Real data only" shows TSLA as Capital.com serves it.
Readouts. "Newest" is the line at the newest candle. The daily line's own value for today is shown beside it; a red number means the panel disagrees with it.
