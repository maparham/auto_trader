Charts, backtests and alerts in one trading workspace

Chartkar runs in your browser. Chart any market, test your trading rules on years of history, screen many symbols at once, and get an alert the moment your setup appears.

Open the live demo Create a free account

The demo needs no sign-up. It uses free market data from Yahoo Finance.

Fast, clean charts

Candlestick charts from 1 minute to 1 month, with the indicators and drawing tools you expect, and a few you will not find elsewhere.

  • Moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, VWAP and many more
  • Pin any indicator to a higher timeframe, such as a 4 hour EMA on a 5 minute chart
  • Automatic trendlines and candle pattern markers
  • Bar replay, heatmaps and multi-chart layouts in tabs

Backtest your rules before you risk money

Write entry and exit rules in plain expressions, such as close crosses above EMA(50), and run them on the chart in seconds. Every trade is drawn where it happened.

  • Net profit, drawdown, win rate, Sharpe, profit factor and more
  • Spread and overnight financing costs included
  • Parameter sweeps to compare many settings at once
  • Walk-forward tests to check that a strategy holds up on new data

Alerts that work while you are away

Alerts run on our servers, not in your browser tab. Close your laptop and they still fire.

  • Price levels, indicator values, divergences and trendline breaks
  • Notifications by browser push, sound and Telegram
  • Telegram alerts include a live picture of your chart

Screener
