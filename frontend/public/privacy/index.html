Privacy Policy

Auto Trader (chartkar.app). Last updated 11 September 2026.

Auto Trader is a charting, backtesting and price alerting tool operated by its author as an individual. This page explains what the service stores about you, why, and how to have it removed.

Short version: we store the account details your sign in provider gives us plus the work you create in the app. We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising trackers, and we never ask you for payment details or broker credentials.

Account information

Sign in is handled by Clerk. When you create an account, Clerk stores your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture that Google shares. Auto Trader receives a user identifier and your email address from Clerk so it can tell your data apart from other people's. We never see or store your password.

Data you create in the app

Anything you produce in the app is stored on our server, attached to your user identifier:

  • Chart and workspace settings, saved presets, and drawing state
  • Backtests, parameter sweeps, walk forward runs and their results
  • Cost profiles and other configuration you enter
  • Price alerts you create and the history of alerts that fired
  • Browser push subscriptions, if you enable push notifications
  • Your Telegram chat identifier, if you link a Telegram account for alerts

Market data

Price history and live quotes are fetched from market data providers and cached on our server. That cache is shared across all users and is not personal data.

Logs

The site is served through Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and abuse prevention. Our application server keeps short lived operational logs. Authentication tokens are stripped from those logs before they are written.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no cross site tracking pixels
  • No selling or renting of personal data to anyone
  • No collection of payment card details, bank details or government identifiers
  • No request for your broker account credentials

Third parties we rely on

  • Clerk for authentication and account management
  • Google if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in
  • Cloudflare for hosting, DNS, network protection and email routing
  • Telegram only if you link it to receive alerts
  • Market data providers, which receive requests for instrument data but not your identity

Cookies

The only cookies set are the session cookies Clerk needs to keep you signed in. The app also uses your browser's local storage to remember interface preferences on your own device. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Write to support@chartkar.app to request a copy of your data or to have your account and everything attached to it deleted. Deletion requests are actioned manually and normally completed within 30 days.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be announced inside the app.
